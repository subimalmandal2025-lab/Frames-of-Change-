About Us

Frames of Change is a visual storytelling initiative focused on documenting education, culture, and community transformation. We use film, photography, and narrative documentation to highlight real classroom experiences and underrepresented voices in education systems.

Our Vision

To create a bridge between education and storytelling — where every classroom becomes a living archive of learning, resilience, and change.

Our Projects

🎓 Education Stories

Documenting classroom transformation, student journeys, and teaching practices in underserved communities.

🎥 Community Voices

Capturing lived experiences of parents, teachers, and local leaders through interviews and short films.

📚 Creative Learning Labs

Using visual media, storytelling, and creative tools to improve classroom engagement and learning outcomes.

Our Impact

We aim to make education more visible, meaningful, and participatory. By documenting real classroom change, we support teachers, students, and institutions in reflecting on growth and innovation.
